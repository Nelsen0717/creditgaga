# CreditGaga — 2-minute demo script (English)

**[Open the app — cards spring out of Gaga]**
Hi, we're CreditGaga.
Every wallet in Taiwan holds five to seven credit cards, and every card pays a different bonus. At the counter nobody remembers which one to use. We tap whatever is on top and leave money on the table.

This is my wallet. Seven real cards. Gaga, the little card guy, already knows which card is best for what: TCB for everyday spend, CUBE for dining, but only after I switch its plan in the CUBE app.

**[Tap "I'm about to buy" — point at the drink / or type it]**
I'm at 50 Lan buying bubble tea. I tap.
Claude reads the photo: the shop, the price, the LINE Pay sticker. Claude only reads. The rules engine on the phone does the math. Every number on this screen comes from the bank's official page, with the link and the date we checked it.

The winning card rises. 1.2 percent, 70 cents back. And the one move that gets more: switch CUBE to the dining plan, and this becomes 3 percent.

**[Wallet → add a card → photograph → rub out the number]**
New card in my wallet. I photograph it and rub the number out with my thumb before anything leaves the phone. Gaga matches the card from the artwork alone, never the digits. If it's a card we have never seen, Claude searches the bank's site and writes the rules itself, with sources, and marks it "AI researched" until I confirm.

**[Statement → sample statement]**
Last month's statement. One photo. One number: 708 dollars left on the table. Times twelve, that's a return flight to Tokyo. And the three things to fix, ranked by how much each one is worth.

**[Close]**
That's CreditGaga. Point, tap, pay with the right card. Traditional Chinese and English, one HTML file, no server, and it saves every statement so next month it can show you what changed.
Thank you.
